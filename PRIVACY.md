# Triage Lens — Privacy Policy
Triage Lens is a Forge app for Jira. It does not collect, store, or share any personal data.
- The app reads Jira issue data (title, status, priority, assignee, comments, linked Confluence pages and Bitbucket pull requests) at runtime through Atlassian APIs, solely to render the triage panel inside the issue. Nothing is stored outside Atlassian.
- No cookies, no tracking, no third-party analytics, no advertising.
